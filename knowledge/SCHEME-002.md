# SCHEME-002 — Senior Citizen Support Pension

**Who it's for:** Retired seniors aged 65+ with limited retirement savings and no active full-time employment.

**Benefit:** A monthly pension payment plus access to discounted healthcare and utility support.

**Eligibility rules:**
- [ ] Applicant is aged 65 or older.
- [ ] Annual income is below the senior support threshold.
- [ ] Not currently receiving a full private retirement income that exceeds eligibility limits.
- [ ] Resident status confirmed for the required minimum period.

**How to apply:**
1. Fill in the Senior Citizen Support Pension application form.
2. Provide proof of age, residency, pension history, and recent bank statements.
3. Include a statement of current income and living expenses.
4. Submit the application to the local social services office or online portal.
