# SCHEME-003 — Small Farm Income Support

**Who it's for:** Small-scale farmers operating family-owned farms who need temporary income support during seasonal shortfalls.

**Benefit:** Quarterly support payment and access to a low-cost agricultural advisory service.

**Eligibility rules:**
- [ ] Farm is classified as small-scale under the local agricultural plan.
- [ ] Annual farm income falls below the program support ceiling.
- [ ] Applicant demonstrates active farm management and contribution to household income.
- [ ] Must submit a basic farm plan and recent production records.

**How to apply:**
1. Complete the Small Farm Income Support application form.
2. Attach proof of farm ownership or lease, income records, and a farm plan.
3. Provide details of household members who rely on farm income.
4. Send the application to the rural assistance office or upload it through the agriculture portal.
