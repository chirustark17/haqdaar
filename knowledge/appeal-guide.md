# Appeal Guide — Benefit Application Rejection

**Who it's for:** Applicants whose benefit application was declined and who need guidance on the appeal process.

**What it explains:** Steps to review the decision, collect supporting documents, and submit a formal appeal within the deadline.

**Appeal steps:**
1. Review the rejection letter carefully, noting the reason and appeal deadline.
2. Gather any additional evidence that addresses the reason for denial.
3. Write a clear appeal statement explaining why the decision should be reconsidered.
4. Submit the appeal through the designated appeals portal or office, including all supporting documents.
5. Track the appeal status and be ready to provide further information if requested.

**Practical tips:**
- Keep copies of all correspondence and documents.
- Note the appeal deadline and submit early.
- If available, ask a caseworker or community advice service for help reviewing your appeal package.
