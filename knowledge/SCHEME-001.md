**Eligibility rules:**
- [ ] Must be enrolled full-time in an approved undergraduate or diploma course.
- [ ] Household income must fall below the published low-income threshold.
- [ ] No existing scholarship or student support payment that fully covers living costs.
- [ ] Must have a valid student ID and proof of enrollment.

**How to apply:**
1. Complete the Student Opportunity Grant application form online.
2. Upload proof of enrollment, recent household income statement, and a copy of your student ID.
3. Submit a short personal statement describing your financial need.
